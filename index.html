ABOUT THE 12 PRINCIPLES

The 12 basic principles of animation were developed by the legendary animators of Walt Disney Studios, amongst them Frank Thomas and Ollie Johnston, during the 1930s. They were published in their seminal 1981 book The Illusion of Life: Disney Animation.

These principles emerged as a result of deep reflection on their craft and Walt Disney’s desire to use animation to express genuine character, personality, and emotion.

This visual gallery demonstrates those timeless 12 principles applied to simple geometric shapes — a cube — illustrating the fundamental mechanics of motion.
